Privacy, Terms & Accessibility

NEXUS 2026 is a fictional event built as a front-end portfolio project.

Privacy Policy

This site has no backend. Nothing you type is sent to a server.

  • Registration details, saved agenda sessions and newsletter emails are stored in your browser’s local storage, on your device only. Clearing your browser data removes them.
  • The contact form opens your own email app with a pre-filled message. Nothing is sent until you press send there.
  • The site loads fonts, icons and the map from third-party services (Google Fonts, jsDelivr, Google Maps) and images from Unsplash and Pravatar. Those services may log your IP address according to their own policies.

Terms of Service

  • NEXUS 2026 is a demonstration project. No tickets are sold, no payments are taken and no event takes place.
  • Speakers, sessions, prices and sponsors are fictional. Any resemblance to real people or events is coincidental.
  • The Javits Center is a real venue and is used here only as a location example. It is not affiliated with this project.
  • The source code is available on GitHub.

Accessibility

The site is built to be usable by everyone. It includes a skip-to-content link, keyboard-friendly navigation, visible focus states, labelled form fields with clear error messages, and respects the “reduce motion” setting of your device.
